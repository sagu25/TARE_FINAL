# TARE — Landing Page Speaker Script
### For Live Presentation · Single Speaker Format
*Tone: Authoritative. Measured. Never salesy. Let the weight of the problem do the work.*

---

## BEFORE YOU SPEAK

**What is on screen:** The TARE landing page — dark background, glowing cyan grid nodes, the TARE logo, maturity cards, monitor parameters, agent ticker scrolling at the bottom.

**Your job on this page:** Make the audience feel the problem before you show the solution. By the time they click Launch Demo, they should already believe the gap is real.

**Do not rush. Do not click anything. Just speak.**

---

## THE SCRIPT

---

### OPENING — Set the Stage
*(Stand still. Let the screen breathe. Start slowly.)*

> "Every time electricity moves from a power station to a building — a hospital, a data centre, a factory — it passes through a network of equipment that needs to be controlled in real time.
>
> For most of history, a human engineer did that. They walked to a substation. They made a decision. They flipped a switch.
>
> Then software did it. Automated rules — if voltage drops, open this breaker. Fast. Predictable. No human needed for routine operations.
>
> And now we are entering a third era."

*(Pause. Let that land.)*

> "AI agents. You give the system a goal. Not a script. Not a rule set. A goal. The agent reasons through the problem, chooses its own steps, decides what to touch and when — and acts. Without a human approving every command.
>
> That is where we are heading. That shift — from human, to automated, to autonomous — is already underway in power grids, water systems, and industrial plants around the world."

---

### THE MATURITY CARDS
*(Point to the three cards on screen: MANUAL → AUTOMATED → AUTONOMOUS)*

> "Look at these three stages.
>
> Level one — a person physically does it. Every action is human-approved by definition. Slow. Safe.
>
> Level two — automation. Software executes pre-written rules. Fast. But rigid. It can only do what it was told to expect.
>
> Level three — autonomous AI. The agent decides. It adapts. It reasons through novel situations. It acts at machine speed. Nobody is holding the wheel.
>
> You'll notice the badge on level three says 'You Are Here.' Because this is the direction the industry is moving right now. Not in five years. Now."

*(Pause.)*

> "And here is the problem.
>
> The security tools we have — they were designed for level one and level two. They answer one question: **Is this identity valid?** If yes, the agent gets in. It can act.
>
> Nobody watches what it does after the door opens.
>
> That blind spot did not matter when a human was making every decision. It did not matter much when a script was following fixed rules. But an autonomous AI agent reasons, adapts, and makes novel choices at machine speed. If its credentials are stolen — or if it is hijacked mid-session — nobody is watching what it does after authentication passes."

---

### THE MONITORS — What TARE Watches
*(Move to the six monitor tiles on screen)*

> "So what should you watch? TARE checks six things on every single command before it reaches the grid.
>
> **Zone boundary** — is this agent operating inside its authorised zone? An agent cleared for Zone 3 has no business touching Zone 1.
>
> **Asset health** — is there even a legitimate reason to be here? An agent shouldn't touch a healthy zone with no fault.
>
> **Safety procedure** — did the agent follow protocol? In a real grid you never open a breaker without running a safety simulation first.
>
> **Burst rate** — how many commands in the last ten seconds? Ten commands in five seconds across three zones looks nothing like normal grid operations. It looks like an attack.
>
> **Behavioural pattern** — the machine learning layer. Even if every individual command looks innocent, the full session pattern can reveal reconnaissance. Slow, quiet reads across every asset in sequence. That is exactly what attackers do before striking.
>
> **Identity fingerprint** — is this actually the agent it claims to be? A forged credential will not match the registered fingerprint. Blocked before a single command gets through."

*(Brief pause.)*

> "When two or more of these checks fire simultaneously — TARE does not wait for a human to notice. It acts immediately."

---

### THE STATS
*(Point to the four stat tiles)*

> "Four attack scenarios. Thirteen specialised AI agents working inside TARE. A real large language model making genuine autonomous decisions — not a simulation, not a pre-scripted sequence. And fully autonomous response — TARE decides, BARRIER enforces, incidents raise themselves, and a human gets a plain-English briefing telling them exactly what happened and what to do next."

---

### THE AGENT TICKER
*(Let the scrolling ticker draw attention naturally)*

> "Those are the thirteen agents you are about to see. Each one has a single responsibility. None of them have unilateral authority. KORAL records. MAREA detects. TASYA adds context. NEREUS advises. BARRIER is the only one who can issue an ALLOW or DENY — and only when TARE tells it to.
>
> Every agent wakes when called, does its job, and goes back to sleep. You will see each one activate in real time during the demo."

---

### THE CLOSE — Hand Off to the Demo
*(This is your push moment. Keep it clean.)*

> "What you are about to see is a working proof of concept.
>
> A real AI agent, making its own decisions, attempting six different attack types — some obvious, some extremely quiet — against a simulated power grid.
>
> Three defence layers watching in parallel. Thirteen agents ready.
>
> And a human supervisor who stays in control of every final decision — never taken out of the loop, never overwhelmed by noise.
>
> The gap is real. The threat is coming. This is what the answer looks like."

*(Click Launch Demo.)*

## HANDLING INTERRUPTIONS

**If someone asks a question during the landing page:**
> "Hold that — I will come back to it after the first scenario. It will make more sense with something on screen."

**If someone says "just show us the demo":**
> "Give me sixty more seconds — the problem is what makes the demo matter."

**If someone asks "is this live or mocked":**
> "The AI agent is fully live — genuine LLM reasoning, no scripted paths. The grid itself is in-memory for the demo. I'll be transparent about exactly what is and isn't wired to production when we get there."
